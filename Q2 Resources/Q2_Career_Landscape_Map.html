How to Start — Pick Your Spark

If you care about constitutional law

Read Supreme Court cases. Study the Fourth Amendment. Understand how technology changes what "privacy" means. Consider law school with a focus on constitutional or civil rights.

If you care about workers & fairness

Talk to people doing gig work. Volunteer with worker advocacy groups. Study labor history and current labor law. Write about what you learn.

If you care about community justice

Get involved locally against surveillance or harmful algorithms. Use art or organizing. Connect with civil rights groups. Show up where the harm is happening.

If you're fascinated by how it all works

Study the intersection of culture, power, and technology. Learn how algorithms shape what we see and believe. Read widely. Travel. Talk to people everywhere.
