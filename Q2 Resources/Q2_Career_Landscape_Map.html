Power, Law & Civic Life
Career Landscape Map

Careers where law, technology, and civic life meet  ·  For students navigating what's next
Student-Created Podcast pathsofcuriosity.com

AI isn't replacing lawyers, advocates, or public servants. It's changing what they do, and raising new questions about rights, power, and fairness. The most important roles will belong to people who can think across law, technology, and human impact at once. This map shows where those careers live, what they require, and how to start exploring them now.

How to Start — Pick Your Spark

If you love advocacy + justice

Join mock trial or debate. Read a summary of a landmark privacy case. Volunteer with a community or civil rights organization. Consider pre-law with a data or tech minor.

If you love legal tech + data

Learn Python and basic text analysis. Try searching a public records dataset. Read about how courts review AI-assisted evidence. Study logic and statistics.

If you love policy + governance

Follow a local or state technology policy debate. Write a one-page policy memo. Attend a city council meeting. Explore political science or public policy.

If you love research + storytelling

Write an explainer on a recent court ruling. Interview a worker or neighbor about a system that affects them. Start a podcast, blog, or video series.
