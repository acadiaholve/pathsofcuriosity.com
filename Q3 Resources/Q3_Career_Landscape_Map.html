Data, Behavior & Human Systems
Career Landscape Map

Careers where data, behavior, and human systems collide  ·  For students navigating what's next
Student-Created Podcast pathsofcuriosity.com

Data now shapes what we see, who gets a loan, how workers are managed, and even how we behave. The people who study, build, and question these systems come from economics, psychology, anthropology, computer science, law, and more. This map shows where those careers live, what they require, and how to start exploring them now.

How to Start — Pick Your Spark

If you love people + behavior

Observe how people use something at school and ask why. Take psychology or economics. Interview someone about a decision they made. Read about nudges and field experiments.

If you love data + experiments

Learn statistics and basic Python. Run a simple A/B test with friends. Analyze a public dataset (Kaggle, government data). Read about how bias enters models.

If you love power + policy

Study fair lending and algorithmic bias cases. Pick a "smart" product and trace who benefits from its data. Write a short opinion piece on a data policy debate.

If you love communicating ideas

Write an explainer on a surprising research finding. Create a podcast, video, or visual series. Practice translating one data idea per week.
